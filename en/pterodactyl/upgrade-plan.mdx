---

---

### 1. Select Upgrade in Server Subscription

Log in to the client dashboard, open the **Subscription** tab on your server, and click **Upgrade**.

<Frame caption="Upgrade Server">
  ![Upgrade Server](https://id-cdn.aisbirnusantara.com/documentation/Screenshot%202026-03-28%20063754.png)
</Frame>

---

### 2. Select New Plan

Choose the plan tier that fits your resource needs (RAM, CPU, and storage).

<Frame caption="Select Plan">
  ![Select Plan](https://id-cdn.aisbirnusantara.com/documentation/Screenshot%202026-03-28%20063929.png)
</Frame>

---

### 3. Complete Payment

Complete the checkout for the selected plan and duration. Once processed, the server resources will be upgraded automatically without losing your data.
