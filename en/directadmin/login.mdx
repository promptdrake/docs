After purchasing a DirectAdmin Web Hosting plan on **Aisbir Cloud Nusantara**, there are **2 easy methods** to access your DirectAdmin control panel:

---

### Option 1: 1-Click Auto Login via Client Portal (Recommended)

The quickest method to log in without entering your username and password:

1. Log in to the [Aisbir Cloud Client Portal](https://portal.aisbirnusantara.com).
2. In the left navigation menu, click **Services** -> **Manage Directadmin**.
3. Locate your hosting domain in the services table.
4. Click the blue **Login** button under the *Action* column.

<Frame caption="Click the Login button under Manage Directadmin Hosting">
  ![DirectAdmin Portal Login Button](https://id-cdn.aisbirnusantara.com/documentation/directadmin-login-portal-button.png)
</Frame>

You will be seamlessly authenticated and redirected straight into your DirectAdmin dashboard in a new tab!

---

### Option 2: Manual Login Using Credentials from Email

If you prefer logging in directly through the panel URL:

1. Check the email inbox registered with your Aisbir Cloud account.
2. Open the hosting activation email with the subject **"Akses Hosting untuk [yourdomain.com]"**.
3. Inside, you will find your connection credentials:
   - **Domain**: Your hosting domain name
   - **Username**: DirectAdmin username (e.g. `aisbirnusantaraa724`)
   - **Password**: Your hosting account password
   - **Panel URL**: Direct login link (e.g. `https://cleo.aisbirnusantara.com:2222`)
4. Click the **Login ke Panel Hosting** button or open the **Panel URL** in your browser.
5. Enter your **Username** and **Password** from the email, then click **Login**.

<Frame caption="Hosting login credentials in the activation email">
  ![Hosting Login Email](https://id-cdn.aisbirnusantara.com/documentation/directadmin-login-email-details.png)
</Frame>

---

## 💡 Important Tips

- **Store Credentials Securely**: Keep your hosting login details in a secure location.
- **Change Default Password**: We strongly recommend updating your initial password after logging in for the first time.
- **Forgot Password?**: If you forget your password, you can always log in instantly using **Option 1 (1-Click Auto Login via Portal)**.
