---
title: "How to order"
---

Use the Aisbir Cloud portal to choose a service, complete your payment, and check your order status.

<Note>
  Make sure you use an active email address. Your Pterodactyl login details will be sent to your account email after the payment is successfully completed.
</Note>

## 1. Open the Portal and Choose a Product

## 2. Order a Pterodactyl Server

1. Open the [Aisbir Cloud Portal](https://portal.aisbirnusantara.com) and log in to your account.
2. Open the **Product List** menu.
3. Choose the product you want to order: **Pterodactyl Server**, **DirectAdmin Hosting**, or **Domain**.
4. Select **Pterodactyl Server**.
5. Compare the package specifications, then click **Add To Cart** on the package that suits your needs.
6. Open your cart, review the selected package and total price, then proceed to payment.
7. Complete the payment using one of the available payment methods.
8. Once your payment has been confirmed, check your account email for your Pterodactyl access details.

Do not share your email, password, or server access details with anyone. Change your password immediately after your first login.

## 3. Order DirectAdmin Hosting

1. Select **DirectAdmin Hosting** from the **Product List** menu.
2. Choose the hosting package that suits your needs.
3. Click **Select Plan**, then proceed to your cart and complete the payment.
4. Once the payment is successful, your hosting details will be sent to your account email.
5. Use the login button provided in the email or follow the [Login to DirectAdmin](/directadmin/login) guide.

## 4. Order a Domain

1. Select **Domain** from the **Product List** menu.
2. Enter the domain name you want, then click **Search**.
3. Choose an available domain extension and review its registration and renewal prices.
4. Add the domain to your cart, fill in the required information, then proceed to payment.
5. Once the payment is successful, wait for the domain registration process to be completed.

## 5. Check Your Order Email

After your payment has been successfully completed, check your inbox and spam folder. The order email may contain:

- Payment confirmation.
- Details of the purchased service.
- DirectAdmin or Pterodactyl login information.
- Domain information and registration status.

If you have not received the email after your payment has been confirmed, create a ticket through [Support](https://portal.aisbirnusantara.com/dashboard/support) and include your order ID and account email address. Do not send your password through a support ticket.
